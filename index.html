The Weekend

Thursday through Sunday.

Thursday is arrival. Fly direct into MYR or drive six hours from Atlanta. Check in at three. The lagoon pool glows at dusk. First beers on the patio.

Friday is Caledonia — twenty-five minutes south in Pawleys Island. The round the trip is built around. Afternoon is the pool, golden hour, the photograph someone frames. Friday night we cook in — two kitchens, fresh seafood from the inlet, nobody leaves the house.

Saturday is TPC in the morning, the MarshWalk at night. The MarshWalk is a half-mile waterfront boardwalk in Murrells Inlet — eight restaurants, live music, open-air. Dead Dog Saloon. Drunken Jack's. Wicked Tuna. Saturday night runs as long as anyone lets it.

Sunday is checkout at ten. Settle the bet. The goodbyes last a beat longer than they need to. Then the cars leave.
