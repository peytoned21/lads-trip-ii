The House

End of the Rainbow

Beachfront · Pawleys Island Historic District · 7 Bedrooms · 18 Beds · National Register of Historic Places · Circa 1800s

Once the summer parsonage for All Saints Episcopal Church. Original floorboards. Fifteen-foot ceilings. Spanish moss hanging over live oaks in the yard. Twenty-two rocking chairs on a porch the length of the house. On one side — the Atlantic, fifty feet away. On the other — the creek, a private dock, the sunset.

Five minutes to Caledonia. Ten to True Blue. Fifteen to the MarshWalk. You come back from the round and you're home before the coffee's cold. The afternoon isn't lost to a commute. It belongs to the porch.

Linens included. Beach towels included. Kayaks, crabbing gear, outdoor gas boiler for seafood boils — all included. 5.0 rating from 17 reviews. Top 5% of all Airbnb listings.

The Weekend

Thursday through Sunday.

Thursday is arrival. Cross the causeway onto Pawleys Island and find a house that has been there since before the Civil War. Bags inside. First beers on the porch. The rocking chairs fill up. The creek catches the last light.

Friday is Caledonia — five minutes across the causeway. Back by one. The afternoon is the beach, the porch, the kayaks on the creek. Friday night is the boil — the gas burner's on the porch, the shrimp comes from the boats a mile away, everyone eats together at the picnic table on the screened porch looking at the creek at dusk.

Saturday is TPC — twenty-five minutes north. Back by two. The MarshWalk is fifteen minutes away. Saturday night is Dead Dog Saloon, Wicked Tuna, the boardwalk at sunset. Short Uber home.

Sunday is checkout. Coffee on the porch. The ocean fifty feet away. The rocking chairs creak. Then the cars leave.
